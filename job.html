Merchandiser

FMCG Distribution Co.  ·  Lagos, Nigeria  ·  Posted August 25, 2026

About the role

We're looking for a field-based Merchandiser to manage product visibility and stock availability across retail outlets in Lagos. You'll be the on-ground link between our distribution team and the shelf — making sure our brands are stocked, priced correctly, and displayed to standard.

What you'll do

  • Visit assigned retail outlets on a fixed route plan
  • Ensure products are fully stocked and correctly displayed
  • Report stock-outs, pricing issues, and competitor activity
  • Build working relationships with store owners and staff

What you'll need

  • OND/HND in any field — Marketing or Business an advantage
  • 1+ years in merchandising, sales, or retail (FMCG a plus)
  • Comfortable on your feet and moving between outlets daily
  • Based in or willing to relocate to Lagos

What's on offer

  • Competitive monthly salary + performance incentives
  • Conveyance provided
  • Career growth opportunities
  • Opportunity to build FMCG sales experience
